# NoteDrift — Campaign Calendar (Tool Factory 1)

> **Operating doc, not a strategy essay.** A rolling ~8-week plan for promoting the
> eight Tool Factory 1 tools on a **Tool-of-the-Week** cadence, built for one
> founder with a day job's worth of other work. If a week feels heavy, cut the
> social post before you cut the SEO page — search compounds, a tweet doesn't.
> Starts the week of **Mon Sep 7, 2026**. Revisit at the end of each 8-week block.

## The tools we're featuring

All free, no-signup, in-browser, private-where-true. One is featured per week.

1. **Merge PDF** — combine PDFs, reorder, one download → `/tools/merge-pdf`
2. **Split PDF** — split into parts by page ranges → `/tools/split-pdf`
3. **Extract PDF Pages** — pick specific pages into a new PDF → `/tools/extract-pdf-pages`
4. **PDF to JPG** — render pages to JPG images → `/tools/pdf-to-jpg`
5. **Crop Image** — crop JPG/PNG/WebP with ratio presets → `/tools/crop-image`
6. **QR Code Generator** — text/URL to QR, PNG, no tracking → `/tools/qr-code-generator`
7. **Word Counter** — words/chars/sentences/reading time → `/tools/word-counter`
8. **Signature Maker** — draw a signature, transparent PNG → `/tools/signature-maker`

## Cadence principles (read before you commit to anything)

- **One SEO page + one short + ~2 social posts per week.** That's the ceiling, not the floor. No daily posting.
- **Batch, don't drip.** Record all eight shorts in one or two sittings; queue social with a scheduler. The calendar is a promise to your future self, not a live obligation.
- **SEO is the real channel.** Every featured tool ships a supporting how-to page that outlives the launch week. Social and video drive the first spike; search carries the tail.
- **Newsletter is opt-in only.** We send to the Resend opt-in list, never auto-opt-in, and only when there's real new content. Most weeks: no send. That's correct.
- **Product Hunt is a one-time card.** It's reserved for the full **bundle launch**, not spent on individual tools. Do not "soft launch" a single tool there.
- **Honest numbers only.** We don't invent search volume and we don't set targets against metrics we can't collect. Checkpoints are "log the baseline and compare week-over-week," not "hit N."

## Recurring monthly beats

**"New on NoteDrift" (monthly, opt-in list).**
End-of-month recap of what actually shipped that month — one line and one link per tool. September = the four PDF tools; October = the image/QR/writing tools + the bundle. Keep it to a 30-second read. If nothing new shipped, skip the month; an empty "here's our newsletter" send trains people to unsubscribe.

**"3 free tools worth bookmarking" (roughly every ~6 weeks, opt-in list).**
A curated pick, not a product announcement — the framing is "here are things useful enough to keep in a bookmarks folder," and one of them happens to be ours-adjacent context. First send in Week 6 (QR + Word Counter + a PDF tool). This is the list-warming send that keeps opens healthy between recaps.

**Product Hunt — the bundle launch (reserved, one shot).**
Do **not** launch individual tools on PH. Hold it for a single "**NoteDrift Free Tools — the no-signup PDF & image toolkit**" launch once all eight are live and stable (target: end of this block, Week 8 or the first clean Tuesday after). One launch, one asset set (gallery GIF per tool, a clear first comment, a "why no signup / why private" angle), a heads-up to the opt-in list the morning of. Launch criteria before we spend the card:
- All 8 tools live, fast, and bug-free on the prod host.
- The tools index page reads as one coherent product, not a link dump.
- Analytics/events confirmed working so we can actually read the traffic spike.
- A day you can babysit comments for ~8 hours. If you can't, move it — a half-attended PH launch is worse than none.

## Production workflow (how a solo founder actually ships this)

- **One batch day per block.** Record all 8 shorts back-to-back (same screen-record setup, same 15–25s framing: problem → three clicks → download). Rough-cut, caption, done.
- **Write SEO pages one week ahead.** The featured tool's how-to should be published and indexing *before* its social week, so the posts point at a live, crawlable page.
- **Queue social, don't post live.** Two posts/week × 8 = 16 items; write them in one sitting and schedule. Leave room for one genuine, unscheduled reply when a real thread comes up.
- **Repurpose every short 4 ways.** One capture → YouTube Short + TikTok + Reel + an X/LinkedIn video. Same file, platform-native captions. No re-shoots.
- **If a week slips, drop social first, then video. Never drop the SEO page** — it's the only asset that keeps working after the week ends.

## What we can and can't measure

**Can collect (use these):**
- Search Console — impressions, clicks, average position, and the actual queries per tool page. This is the primary scoreboard.
- Web/product analytics — pageviews and (where instrumented) tool-run events per tool.
- Resend — newsletter opens, clicks, unsubscribes, list size (opt-in only).
- Pro billing — conversions and plan, via the server-authoritative billing already in place.

**Won't fabricate:** keyword search volumes (write "needs research (Search Console)"), competitor numbers, "expected reach," or any funnel metric we haven't wired up. A checkpoint is "record the baseline, compare next week" — never a made-up target. If we can't measure it, we don't report it.

## Guardrails (non-negotiable)

- **Voice:** useful, smart, energetic, clean, a little playful. "Open. Think. Create." For the tools: "Useful tools. Fast. Private where technically true. No signup where possible."
- **Never:** fake urgency, fake scarcity, dark patterns, "revolutionary/game-changing/disrupt," emoji spam, or corporate filler.
- **Privacy claims must be literally true.** Say "files never leave your device / processed in your browser" only where that is exactly how the tool works. No hand-wavy "private" on anything that isn't.
- **Email is opt-in, always.** No purchased lists, no auto-subscribe, one-click unsubscribe honored. Marketing sends go only to people who asked for them.
